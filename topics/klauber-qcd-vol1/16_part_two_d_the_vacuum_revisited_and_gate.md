## Why this matters

Read critically: **Klauber's zero-point-energy cancellation position is his own research programme, interesting and not consensus.** The module is as much about distinguishing three claims (what QFT predicts, what Klauber argues, what evidence would separate them) as about the physics. It also closes Part Two with the compression page.

## Prerequisites

Modules 07 and 13 to 15 (the $\delta^3(0)$ volume term; normal ordering).

## Detailed topics (checklist)

- Zero-point energy of a free field: $\rho_{\rm vac}=\frac12\int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\omega_{\mathbf k}\to\frac{\Lambda^4}{16\pi^2}$ for a massless scalar with a sharp cutoff; for a Dirac field $-4\times\frac12\omega$ per mode (negative: fermion loops); bosons and fermions can cancel if degrees of freedom match (supersymmetry-like) but they do not in the Standard Model.
- **The observable vacuum:** the Casimir effect (the dependence of vacuum energy on boundary conditions) and the Lamb shift show *differences* of vacuum energy are physical; the absolute zero-point energy gravitates, giving the cosmological-constant problem.
- **Klauber's position (state it from the text):** zero-point energy is argued to cancel in a consistent treatment (his arXiv papers); *mainstream position:* the absolute vacuum energy is an unresolved issue (it is not simply removed by normal ordering because gravity couples to $T^{\mu\nu}$), with the observed $\rho_\Lambda\approx(2.3\ {\rm meV})^4$.

## Exercises

1. **[T] Cutoff estimate:** $\frac12\int^\Lambda\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}k=\frac{1}{4\pi^2}\int_0^\Lambda k^3dk=\frac{\Lambda^4}{16\pi^2}$; with $\Lambda=M_P=1.22\times10^{19}$ GeV this is $\approx1.4\times10^{74}$ GeV$^4$.
2. **[T] The mismatch:** the observed dark-energy density $\rho_\Lambda\approx(2.3\times10^{-12}\ {\rm GeV})^4=2.8\times10^{-47}$ GeV$^4$, a ratio of $\sim5\times10^{120}$ to the Planck-cutoff estimate.
3. **[T] Casimir pressure:** ideal plates at separation $d$: $P=-\frac{\pi^2\hbar c}{240d^4}$; at $d=1\ \mu$m, $|P|=1.3\times10^{-3}$ Pa ($1.3$ mPa).
4. **[T] The cancellation requirement:** boson and fermion zero-point energies cancel iff $\sum_{\rm bosons}g_B=\sum_{\rm fermions}g_F$ (and masses equal for the leading term); the Standard Model has $g_B=28$ and $g_F=90$ at high temperature, so no exact cancellation.
5. **[T] Normal ordering and gravity:** show $:T^{00}:$ vanishes in the vacuum but argue why a gravitational field couples to $\langle T^{\mu\nu}\rangle$ before normal ordering is chosen.
6. **[T] Lamb-shift link:** the vacuum fluctuation picture gives a shift $\sim\alpha^5m$ ($\approx1058$ MHz for the $2S_{1/2}$-$2P_{1/2}$ splitting in hydrogen): an observable vacuum effect.
7. **[T] Volume divergence:** $E_0=V\rho_{\rm vac}$ with $\rho_{\rm vac}$ an energy density: the infinite term is extensive and does not affect energy *differences* in flat space.
8. **[T] Dimension count:** $\rho_{\rm vac}\sim\Lambda^4$ for any cutoff; in dimensional regularisation the massless contribution vanishes and the massive one is $\propto m^4\ln m^2$.
9. **[C] Positions:** state Klauber's position on zero-point cancellation, then the mainstream position, then what evidence would distinguish them.
10. **[C] What is the vacuum?** Explain in a paragraph, without equations, what the vacuum state is in QFT and what does and does not fluctuate.
