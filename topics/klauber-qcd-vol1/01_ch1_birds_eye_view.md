# Module 01: Chapter 1, Bird's Eye View (Week 1; orientation)

**Books:** Klauber Ch. 1; for the second source, Tong's first lecture or Lancaster and Blundell's introductory chapters.
**Time:** 1 week.

## Why this matters

The most precise available measurement of where the first pass stands is the **diff between your own map of QFT and Klauber's.** Write the map first, from memory, then read his and compare. You have thirteen chapters of material to draw on; the places where your map is thin or confused are the places the rest of this plan targets.

## Diagnostic (30 minutes, no notes)

1. Draw (in objects, operations and goals) a map of QFT from memory: what objects exist (fields, states, operators, propagators, amplitudes), what operations connect them, and what the goal is.
2. Convert $1$ GeV$^{-1}$ to fm and $1$ fm$^{-1}$ to MeV using $\hbar c=197.327$ MeV fm.
3. Give the mass dimension of $\phi$, $\psi$, $A_\mu$ and the coupling $\lambda$ in $\frac\lambda{4!}\phi^4$ in four spacetime dimensions with $\hbar=c=1$.
4. State what goes wrong with relativistic single-particle quantum mechanics (three items).
5. State the fine-structure constant to four figures.

## Prerequisites

None.

## Detailed topics (checklist)

- The conceptual arc of Volume 1: classical fields $\to$ canonical quantisation $\to$ free fields (scalar, spinor, vector) $\to$ interactions (S-matrix, Dyson series, Wick, Feynman rules) $\to$ renormalization $\to$ application to experiment; where Volume 2 picks up.
- Natural units $\hbar=c=1$: dimensions; the Klauber conventions flagged for later modules (relativistic versus non-relativistic state normalisation; discrete versus continuum normalisation).
- The failures of single-particle relativistic QM: negative-energy solutions, a non-positive-definite density for the Klein-Gordon equation, and the fact that particle number is not conserved in relativistic processes (pair creation); all three are cured by quantised fields.

## Exercises

1. **[C] The map:** draw the bird's-eye map of QFT before reading, then correct it; list three things on your map that Klauber's map shows as connected and yours did not.
2. **[C] Why a field theory?** Why does a *field* theory exist at all? What goes wrong with relativistic single-particle QM that forces it?
3. **[T] Unit conversions:** $1\ {\rm GeV}^{-1}=0.1973$ fm; $1\ {\rm fm}^{-1}=197.3$ MeV; the proton Compton wavelength $\frac{1}{m_p}=0.2103$ fm; the electron Compton wavelength $\frac{\hbar}{m_ec}=386$ fm.
4. **[T] Dimensions in 3+1 dimensions:** $[\phi]=1$, $[\psi]=\frac32$, $[A_\mu]=1$, $[\lambda]=0$ for $\lambda\phi^4$ and $[e]=0$ for QED (mass dimension), from $[\mathcal L]=4$.
5. **[T] Dimensions in other dimensions:** in $d=2+1$ spacetime dimensions $[\phi]=\frac12$ and $\lambda\phi^4$ has $[\lambda]=1$; state what that means for renormalizability (preview).
6. **[T] $\alpha$:** $\alpha=\frac{e^2}{4\pi}=\frac1{137.036}=7.297\times10^{-3}$ (Heaviside-Lorentz natural units); the Rydberg energy $\frac12\alpha^2m_e=13.6$ eV.
7. **[C] Negative probability:** state, without equations, why a conserved density that is not positive-definite cannot be a probability density, and where $\bar\psi\psi$ appears in its place later.
8. **[C] Particle number:** give a physical process in which the particle number changes and explain why no fixed-$N$ wave-function theory can describe it.
9. **[C] Scope:** list five things Volume 1 will *not* cover and one that Volume 2 will.
10. **[C] Explain-it paragraph:** one paragraph, no equations, saying what QFT is for a competent physicist who has not studied it.

## Optional extension

Read Tong's lecture 1 and note where his map differs from Klauber's; record any difference that is a convention versus a substantive disagreement.

## Pitfalls

- Reading Klauber's map first and "recognising" it instead of writing yours.
- Confusing the dimensions of fields in $d\neq4$.
- Treating natural units as mysterious (they are bookkeeping for $\hbar$ and $c$).

## Gate questions

1. What are the three failures of relativistic single-particle QM?
2. Which objects does your map link that Klauber's links differently?
3. Where does Volume 1 end and Volume 2 begin?
