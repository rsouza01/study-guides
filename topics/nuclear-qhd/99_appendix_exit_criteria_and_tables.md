# Appendix: exit criteria, correspondence table, exclusions, parked material and references

> Section numbers and book descriptions are from memory. Verify them against your copies.

## 1. Exit criteria (after the 29 weeks, cold)

1. Construct outer-crust composition and EOS from a mass table, explaining every term.
2. Derive the tensor force from pion exchange and the sign rule for scalar versus vector exchange, including the Yukawa derivation from scratch.
3. Explain why the independent-particle model works despite a hard core; derive HF variationally.
4. Classify a nucleus as vibrational or rotational from its spectrum; explain the moment-of-inertia deficit.
5. Derive the fission deformation energy and fissility; connect them to the pasta instability (**with the two relations kept straight**).
6. Compute a Gamow factor, an allowed $\beta$ spectrum, an $ft$ value and a Weisskopf estimate; know which selection rules apply.
7. Locate the Gamow peak; explain pycnonuclear fusion and deep crustal heating.
8. Write $E/A(n,\delta)$, define the five numbers, state what each controls.
9. Derive neutron drip; construct the inner crust; locate the crust-core transition and its $L$ dependence.
10. Write the QHD Lagrangian from memory, perform the mean-field reduction, derive the EOS and **explain relativistic saturation without notes**.
11. Extend to asymmetric matter with the $\rho$; compute $J$ and $L$; impose $\beta$ equilibrium; get the Urca threshold *and* rate.
12. Build a thermodynamically consistent unified EOS and integrate it with TOV.
13. Explain what the envelope does and why surface temperature is not interior temperature.
